# HeroNecromancer — Story Bible (GRRM-Inspired Dark Fantasy)

## 1. Core Premise: The Inverted Light
* **The Protagonist (The Sun-Touched / The Bonachón)**: Once a beloved, genial hero chosen by the Sun Citadel. After felling the Demon Lord alongside his sworn squad, he returned not to a crown, but to poisoned chalices and royal halberds.
* **The Betrayal**: King Alden feared that a living god-slayer commander would render the royal bloodline obsolete. The vanguard was butchered in the banquet hall; their corpses were dumped into the Charnel Trench.
* **The Inversion**: In grief and despair, the Hero's pure Holy Light inverted. Unable to heal mortal flesh, his light now rebinds souls to bone—creating **Pale Necromancy** (radiant white flames binding loyal spirits).

---

## 2. The Fallen Vanguard (The Starter Deck Companions)
* **Vargas (The Iron Bastion)**: Veteran shield-bearer who treated the Hero like a son. Even in skeletal decay, his spirit stands between the Hero and incoming blades.
* **Kaelen (The Windseeker)**: Cynical, razor-tongued scout who warned against trusting the King. Returns as a vengeful spectral marksman.
* **Lyra (The Morning Bell)**: Pure-hearted acolyte whose hymns once soothed kingdoms. Slain clutching her holy symbol; her soul now fuels dark wards and soul siphons.

---

## 3. Major Factions
* **The Royal House of Alden**: Rulers of the High Citadel. Paranoid, ruthless pragmatists who eliminate popular heroes to safeguard feudal stability.
* **The Sun Citadel & Radiant Inquisitors**: Holy clerics who declare the resurrected Hero an abomination, ignoring the King’s treachery.
* **The Discarded**: The restless dead, peasants, and deserters trapped in the kingdom’s charnel trenches.

---

## 4. Narrative Design Principles
* **Tragic Benevolence**: The Hero is still fundamentally warmhearted (*bonachón*), but broken. He does not view himself as an evil lich; he is giving justice and unbroken brotherhood to those the Crown betrayed.
* **Cards as Memories & Oaths**: Every combat card is an echo of an oath sworn, a squad drill, or a warped miracle.
