Log of established narrative events, player decisions, and story continuity across chapters.

## Prologue: The Charnel Trench
* **State**: Scripted (`intro_scene` in `dialog.gd`)
* **Setting**: The Banquet of the High Citadel (Flashback) $\rightarrow$ The Charnel Trench beneath the capital walls.
* **Core Events**:
  - The Hero and his Vanguard celebrate the Demon Lord's demise before King Alden.
  - The King poisons their wine and commands royal halberdiers to butcher the Vanguard.
  - The Hero awakens discarded in a mass grave, surrounded by rotting soldiers and his butchered companions.
  - In agony, his Holy Light warps into Pale Necromancy, knitting his comrades' bones and granting his starter deck.
* **Key Characters**:
  - **The Hero**: Former Sun-Touched paladin, now the Pale Necromancer.
  - **Vargas**: Fallen shieldbearer raised as an undying sentinel card.
  - **Kaelen**: Slain scout raised as an ethereal sniper card.
  - **Lyra**: Murdered priestess whose inverted benedictions provide warding cards.
  - **King Alden**: The architect of the betrayal.
* **Current Status**:
  - Vanguard bound to the Hero's will.
  - Objective: Fight out of the Charnel Trench and march toward the High Citadel.

## Chapter 1: The Bread & The Scythe
* **State**: In Development (`dialog/data/chapter_1.json`)
* **Setting**: Oakhaven Farmlands & Granary Outposts.
* **Core Events**:
  - The Hero and risen Vanguard escape the Charnel Trench and emerge into the kingdom's agrarian borders.
  - Terrified peasants, incited by royal criers and church bells claiming an abominable necromancer is hunting commoners, arm themselves with scythes and pitchforks.
  - Moral conflict: The *bonachón* Hero is forced to battle the innocent commoners he sacrificed everything to save.
* **Key Characters**:
  - **The Hero**: Desperately trying to negotiate before being forced into combat.
  - **Vargas / Vanguard**: Defending the Hero against pitchfork mobs while questioning the cost of his mercy.
  - **Peasant Militia**: Terrified farmers defending their harvest and families.
* **Resolution & Psychological Toll**:
  - The Hero routs the militia without slaughtering them, refusing King Alden's framing.
  - **First Necrotic Toll**: Tasting captured rye bread, the Hero realizes he can no longer taste food—only ash and cold iron. His hands radiate perpetual frost.
  - **Act I Status**: The Hero remains an agonizing idealist, desperate to hold onto his moral code, but the necrotic corruption has begun claiming his mortal senses.
* **Next Destination**: Chapter 2 — The Mourning Crags & Church Inquisition Bastions.

## Chapter 2: The Mourning Crags
* **State**: Scripted (`dialog/data/chapter_2.json`, `world_map/data/ch2_map_data.tres`)
* **Setting**: The Gallows Pass & High Inquisition Bastion in the obsidian mountain peaks.
* **Core Events**:
  - The Hero and Vanguard ascend into the frozen peaks, passing iron gibbets filled with heretics executed by King Alden's decree.
  - Grand Judiciar Vane and the golden-crested Templar Crusade barricade the pass with pikes, braziers, and holy oil.
  - Confrontation with former peers: Vane was present at the betrayal banquet and stood silent as the Vanguard was poisoned.
  - **The Turning Point (Act II)**: The Hero ceases pleading. The regret of Chapter 1 transforms into icy, ruthless resolve. He claims the title *"Lord of Bones"* bestowed by Lyra and unleashes the Vanguard in a bloodbath through the pass.
  - **Climax**: Defeat and soul-binding of High Inquisitor Malakor. The Hero mechanically binds the Inquisitor's soul into his grimoire without flinching, marking the complete erosion of his mortal empathy.
* **Key Characters**:
  - **The Hero**: Veins blackened, corpse-fire burning steadily, emotional response flatlined.
  - **Lyra (Spirit)**: Recognizes her former cathedral mentors among the inquisitors; dubs the Hero "Lord of Bones".
  - **Kaelen (Spirit)**: Scouts the mountain ridges, calling out enemy halberd formations.
  - **Vargas (Spirit)**: Reinforces that the nobility viewed them as disposable tools.
  - **Grand Judiciar Vane**: The corrupt judge who sanctioned the execution of the Vanguard.
  - **High Inquisitor Malakor**: Boss commander of the mountain fortress.
* **Next Destination**: Chapter 3 — The High Citadel & The Throne of King Alden.

## Chapter 3: The High Citadel
* **State**: Scripted (`dialog/data/chapter_3.json`, `world_map/data/ch3_map_data.tres`)
* **Setting**: The Royal Capital, Grand Marble Gates, and the Banquet Hall of the High Citadel.
* **Core Events**:
  - The Hero's undead host breaches the gilded marble gates of the capital. Royal halberdiers break and flee.
  - The Vanguard ascends the grand avenue, storming the palace and reaching the exact Banquet Hall where their lives were stolen in the Prologue.
  - King Alden is cornered behind overturned tables of spoiled meat and sour wine. He screams, offers kingdoms, gold, and excuses.
  - **The Chilling Execution**: Without a word or tremor of anger, the Hero reaps Alden with his pale scythe, reanimating the dead king as a mute halberdier sentry stationed at the door.
  - **The Tragic Revelation (Act III)**: The Hero ascends the throne of bone and stone. There is no joy, relief, or vengeance—only cold, hollow numbness.
  - Gazing into the gilded mirror, the Hero sees hollow burning sockets and horned pale flames: he realizes he has taken the Demon Lord's mantle and completed his total loss of humanity.
* **Key Characters**:
  - **The Hero / Lord of Bones**: Completely hollowed of mortal empathy, ascended as the Pale Usurper.
  - **King Alden**: Executed and bound as an undying sentry.
  - **Vargas (Spirit)**: Witnesses the tragic silence of their vengeance.
  - **Lyra (Spirit)**: Confirms the Hero's heartbeat has ceased forever.
* **Campaign Status**: Trilogy Complete — *The Pale Usurper Ending*.
