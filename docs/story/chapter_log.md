## Prologue: The Charnel Trench
* **State**: Scripted (`intro_scene` in `dialog.gd`)
* **Setting**: The Banquet of the High Citadel (Flashback) $\rightarrow$ The Charnel Trench beneath the capital walls.
* **Core Events**:
  - The Hero and his Vanguard celebrate the Demon Lord's demise before King Alden.
  - The King poisons their wine and commands royal halberdiers to butcher the Vanguard.
  - The Hero awakens discarded in a mass grave, surrounded by rotting soldiers and his butchered companions.
  - In agony, his Holy Light warps into Pale Necromancy, knitting his comrades' bones and granting his starter deck.
* **Key Characters**:
  - **The Hero**: Former Sun-Touched paladin, now the Pale Necromancer.
  - **Vargas**: Fallen shieldbearer raised as an undying sentinel card.
  - **Kaelen**: Slain scout raised as an ethereal sniper card.
  - **Lyra**: Murdered priestess whose inverted benedictions provide warding cards.
  - **King Alden**: The architect of the betrayal.
* **Current Status**:
  - Vanguard bound to the Hero's will.
  - Objective: Fight out of the Charnel Trench and march toward the High Citadel.

## Chapter 1: The Bread & The Scythe
* **State**: In Development (`dialog/data/chapter_1.json`)
* **Setting**: Oakhaven Farmlands & Granary Outposts.
* **Core Events**:
  - The Hero and risen Vanguard escape the Charnel Trench and emerge into the kingdom's agrarian borders.
  - Terrified peasants, incited by royal criers and church bells claiming an abominable necromancer is hunting commoners, arm themselves with scythes and pitchforks.
  - Moral conflict: The *bonachón* Hero is forced to battle the innocent commoners he sacrificed everything to save.
* **Key Characters**:
  - **The Hero**: Desperately trying to negotiate before being forced into combat.
  - **Vargas / Vanguard**: Defending the Hero against pitchfork mobs.
  - **Peasant Militia**: Terrified farmers defending their harvest and families.
